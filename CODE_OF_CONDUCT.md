# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Be professional, honest, and direct. Disagree on technical merit, not on people.

## Engineering-specific expectations

- **No silent failure.** Report errors, blocked tasks, and broken assumptions.
- **No fabricated certainty.** If you do not know, say `UNKNOWN`. Do not invent context.
- **Record material conclusions.** Decisions made in chat, calls, or meetings must be
  transferred into the repository (`engineering/` records).
- **Respect the record.** Corrections supersede; they do not erase history.

## Scope

Applies to all contributors — human and AI agents — in all project spaces.
